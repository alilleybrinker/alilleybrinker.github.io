Omarchy is a Linux distribution created and controlled by David Heinemeier Hansson, usually known
as DHH. DHH is the founder and CTO of 37signals (formerly Basecamp) and creator of Ruby on Rails.

In the last year DHH has also called for the ethnic cleansing of Europe.

In ["As I remember London,"][london] he voiced support for ethnically cleansing London of
minorities. In ["Wolves, sheep, and gypsies,"][wolves] he called for the ethnic cleansing of Romani
people across Europe, comparing them to wolves.

He is, in short, an outspoken bigot who supports programs of mass death and forced migration of
minority communities.

This bigotry is also not new. In 2023, [Duke University divested from Basecamp][duke] over prior
events including reporting that Basecamp employees maintained a racially-charged list of "funny"
customer names and DHH's vocal public support for anti-diversity actions by the U.S. Supreme Court
and by other companies.

Today the Omacom Foundation, which financially supports development of Omarchy and is controlled
by DHH, [announced that 1Password has committed $300,000 to them over the next three years][omacom].
1Password is the first corporate sponsor of Omarchy that is not itself owned by DHH. DHH's company
37signals has also committed $300,000; all other named donors are wealthy individuals.

Apparently DHH's support for ethnic cleansing is not disqualifying to 1Password.

I am a longtime user and supporter of 1Password. I pushed my family to adopt it, encouraged friends
to use it, and have promoted it online. The 1Password team were early and vocal adopters of the
Rust programming language, and have done good work in contributing back to the Rust community
with open source libraries.

However, financing bigotry like this is crossing a red line. I've contacted 1Password with my
opposition to this Omacom Foundation commitment, and if it is not walked back, I will migrate my
family off of 1Password and never give them another dollar.

If you are a 1Password user, I recommend you do the same: contact 1Password with your opposition
to this funding for Omarchy, and divest from the company if they do not change course.

If you are a Rustacean working at 1Password, please raise hell within the company about how
unacceptable this is.

If your employer is a corporate customer of 1Password, please raise hell within your company about
the reputational risk of financing support for ethnic cleansing.

Below is the email I sent to 1Password. Feel free to repurpose it for yourself.

There are many worthwhile projects in the Linux ecosystem that would be worthy recipients of
$300,000. Giving it to Omarchy is unacceptable.

